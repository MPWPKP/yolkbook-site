Apps come and go. Your egg records should stay.

When a popular free egg tracker disappeared from the App Store, keepers lost years of laying records with it. Yolkbook is built so that can't happen to you: there is no account to close and no server of ours to shut down.

  • Stored on your phone

    Flocks, eggs, costs, hatches, notes and photos live in the app on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

  • A backup in one tap

    Save one backup file to Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email, and restore it on any phone, iPhone or Android.

  • Plain files you can open

    Export eggs, expenses, birds and hatches as CSV for any spreadsheet, or as monthly and yearly PDF reports.

What you can do with Yolkbook

Six screens cover the daily routine of a small flock, from the morning egg count to a hatch three weeks later.

Log the day in two taps

The Today screen has a big counter for each flock. Open the app, tap +, done.

  • Home screen widget with +1 and −1 for each flock
  • Optional per-hen counts, so you see who is laying
  • Egg marks: double yolk, soft shell, broken and more
  • Track eggs sold, given away and used at home

See every day of the month

A calendar shades each day by how many eggs you collected. Days you forgot to log look different from days with zero eggs, so gaps are easy to fill in.

Know what an egg really costs

Log feed, bedding and other expenses in a few seconds; the app remembers what you bought last time. Yolkbook divides your costs by your eggs.

  • Cost per egg and per dozen, next to your store price
  • Egg sales and a running balance
  • Equipment like a new coop kept out of the cost per egg
  • Your own currency and pack size (6, 10, 12…)

A card for every bird

Name your hens and keep their photos, breed, hatch date and status: laying, broody, molting, not laying. Each bird has a history and its own notes.

  • Notes with dates, tags and photos, in your own words
  • Reminders on your schedule, such as cleaning the coop
  • Location data removed from every photo you add

Hatches on a calendar

Set eggs in the incubator or under a broody hen and Yolkbook lays out the candling days, lockdown and hatch day, with a reminder for each.

  • Hatch rate from eggs set and from fertile eggs
  • Add the hatched chicks to a flock in one tap
  • Typical day counts for six species, always editable

Charts and reports Pro for full history

Charts for eggs, eggs per flock, lay rate, cost per egg, feed per month and your balance, plus how this month compares with the same month last year and how many eggs each named hen laid.

Questions keepers ask

Can I bring my old egg records?

Yes. Import daily egg counts from a spreadsheet or from another app's CSV export. You need a date column and a number of eggs; a flock column is optional.

Can I move from iPhone to Android, or the other way?

Yes. Create a backup file in Settings, save it to cloud storage or email it to yourself, and restore it on the new phone. The file includes your photos.

Two of us look after the hens. Can we share one flock?

Each phone keeps its own records, and there is no live sync yet. You can merge a backup file from another phone; when both phones logged the same day, the newer entry is kept.

Is there a subscription?

No. The free version has no time limit. Pro and Breeder Pack are one-time purchases.

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

The app goes back to the free version. Everything you entered stays, and you can still view and export it.

Does Yolkbook need an internet connection?

No. Everything works offline. The app only connects to the App Store or Google Play when you buy or restore a purchase.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later, and Android phones and tablets with Android 7.0 or later. The interactive widget needs iOS 17 on iPhone.

Does Yolkbook give health or feeding advice?

No. Yolkbook records what you enter. Reference values such as incubation days are typical starting points and you can change them.
