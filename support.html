Support

Write to mpwpkp@gmail.com. The quickest way is in the app: Settings → About → Report a problem — it adds the app version and the number of records, never your flock data. We usually answer within 2 working days.

Backups and changing phones

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Settings → Backup & restore → Create backup. Save the file to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or send it to yourself by e-mail. On the new phone install Flock Log, open Settings → Backup & restore → Restore from file and pick the file. It works between iPhone and Android too.

Are my photos included?

Yes, backup files include photos. Your phone's own Google cloud backup on Android does not include photos, so keep a backup file if your photos matter to you.

I restored the wrong file. Can I undo it?

Yes. Before every restore the app saves an automatic safety copy. Right after restoring tap “Undo restore”, or find the copy in Backup & restore → Automatic copies.

The app says it couldn't open my data.

Nothing has been deleted. On that screen share the data file, the copy from before the update and the error log, and send them to us — we will help you get everything back.

Purchases

I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Settings → Flock Log Pro → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Everything you entered stays and remains visible and exportable. The free version keeps the egg counter, widget, cost per egg, backups and CSV; Pro features such as more flocks, full history and PDF reports need Pro.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play in your order history.

Using the app

How do I add the home screen widget?

iPhone: touch and hold the home screen → Edit → Add Widget → Flock Log. Android: Settings → Your data → Add home screen widget.

Can I import data from a spreadsheet?

Yes: Settings → Import CSV. You need columns with the date and the number of eggs (and optionally the flock).

Does Flock Log give health or feeding advice?

No. It records what you enter. Reference values such as incubation days are typical values and always editable.
